About this book

This book is a modern upgrade in the spirit of Russell’s The Parousia: not a prophecy hobby, not end-times theatre, and not delay-based spirituality. It is written for readers who have been taught to wait, and who are now asking what Scripture actually meant by “near,” “at hand,” and “this generation.”

  • Audience relevance first — texts are read within their original covenant world.
  • Hebrew idiom default — “heaven and earth,” “clouds,” and cosmic imagery are covenant language.
  • Zero futurist drift — no double-fulfillment, no endless postponement.
  • Practical outcome — faith becomes presence, maturity, and wisdom lived now.

If you came here from FP Bible, this book is best read as a supporting study, not as a replacement for chapter-by-chapter commentary.
